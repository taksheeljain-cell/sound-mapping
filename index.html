Sound Map

A Sound Exploration Game

How to Play

🎮 Controls

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move through the maze.

🔊 Objective

Find 3 hidden sound sources (Red Balls) in each level by following audio cues.

👂 Tips

  • Sounds get louder as you approach
  • Red Balls become more visible when nearby
  • Listen carefully to distinguish directions
  • Remember the paths you explore!

📍 Final Challenge

All three mazes merge into one map. Navigate from Start to Finish using your memory of the maze layouts.
